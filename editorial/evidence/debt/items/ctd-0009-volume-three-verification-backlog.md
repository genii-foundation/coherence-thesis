## Debt

The published corpus retains proposed physiological mechanisms and quantitative assumptions whose supporting evidence warrants further investigation. These claims may remain in the manuscripts as statements of possibility, but their scientific, operational, ecological, and financial foundations have not received a complete claim-level review.

## Evidence

Volume III's evidence map names research concerning neurodivergence, polyvagal theory, HRV, synchrony, vocal state, pulse morphology, oxytocin, cortisol, and endocrine inference. The volume also retains a three-to-five-year research horizon, a ten-to-twenty-percent mentorship referral share, ecological boundaries, launch counts, board and capital proportions, participant targets, and financing estimates. Volumes VII and IX retain attributed quotations and etymological or cultural lineages whose primary sources are not fully established. Volume VIII retains volatile quantitative claims and source-family notes rather than a claim-level apparatus. Volume IX presents voice, HRV, pulse, and breath as inputs to a proposed research agenda rather than established measures of truth, wisdom, or moral worth. On 2026-08-17, the author directed that reader-facing production instructions be removed while substantive limitations remain visible. The remaining obligation is deferred until a dedicated claim-level source audit is commissioned with qualified scientific and cultural reviewers; that commissioning and reviewer availability are the conditions for resuming paydown.

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Verify the title, authors, venue, date, methods, population, effect size, limitations, and replication status of each scientific source carried by this obligation.
- C2. Define testable pulse and endocrine hypotheses and obtain qualified clinical and cultural review.
- C3. Build research, ecological, operational, and bottom-up financial models that state assumptions, dependencies, stopping conditions, accessibility, compensation, security, insurance, and contingency.
- C4. Record the findings from later research cycles and revise any retained claim whose force exceeds the evidence.

## Partial paydown

- 2026-08-17: The author approved a resolution candidate that removes bracketed verification markers, internal editorial directives, and before-publication instructions while retaining natural-language limits on evidence, attribution, methods, populations, denominators, and claim scope. The scientific, attribution, and quantitative claims are not declared verified. The ticket is deferred until the dedicated source-audit condition named above is met.
