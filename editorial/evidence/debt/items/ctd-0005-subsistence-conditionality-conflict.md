## Debt

The manuscripts make incompatible promises about material survival. One makes food, shelter, care, dignity, and belonging unconditional. Another ties material provision to self-regulation or a coherence-derived condition.

## Evidence

Volume I states that the currency may never lower the floor of survival. Revised Volume III now states that food, shelter, dignity, and belonging do not depend on physiology, presence, or usefulness, but that doctrinal correction still awaits author ratification and a real provisioning institution. Volume IX calls the floor unconditional, then says people earn it through self-coherence and that "we feed all who can hold themselves."

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Record the author's decision on whether material provision is unconditional or tied to self-regulation.
- C2. Define the capacity-limited participation guarantee, including voluntary entry, voluntary withdrawal, and protection through illness, disability, dependence, caregiving, crisis, technological exclusion, and temporary incapacity.
- C3. Prohibit biometric evidence, measured self-regulation, perceived coherence, productivity, usefulness, accumulated COHERENCE, and moral judgment from establishing or removing provision, membership, equal vote weight, or human worth.
- C4. Place earned stewardship above provision as bounded, contestable responsibility without superior human or political standing.
- C5. State which institution, funding, goods, reserves, jurisdiction, scarcity rules, withdrawal transition, and remedies remain undefined, and prevent any pilot from promising provision until those particulars are published and reviewable.
- C6. Reconcile the affected corpus doctrine, manuscripts, and voice authorities, then complete independent semantic, literary, and slop review.
- C7. Preserve publication safety by identifying every invalidated spoken section and preventing merge until matching immutable audio and the remaining manuscript publication gates pass.

## Resolution

### Outcome

The corpus now distinguishes a destination from a capacity-limited beginning. The destination is a universal unconditional material floor. Until institutions and resources can carry that promise, a Providence consociation may guarantee only provision it can actually supply to people who freely join and genuinely participate in its shared life. Voluntary withdrawal ends that member guarantee. Illness, disability, dependence, caregiving, crisis, technological exclusion, and temporary incapacity do not. No biometric result, self-regulation measure, coherence score, productivity judgment, or claim of usefulness may establish or remove provision, membership, equal vote weight, or human worth. Earned stewardship remains above provision as bounded, reviewable responsibility without superior human or political standing.
