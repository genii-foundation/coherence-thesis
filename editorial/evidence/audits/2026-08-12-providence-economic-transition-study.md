# Providence Economic Transition Study

## Purpose and limit

This study translates the 2026-08-12 CTD-0005 author ruling into a staged institutional research agenda. It does not claim that Providence, a consociation, a Scale, COHERENCE, or a material floor currently operates. It is not legal, tax, securities, employment, medical, privacy, or financial advice. Qualified counsel and affected communities must review any real pilot in its actual jurisdiction before funds, benefits, health data, labor, land, or transferable value move.

## Doctrine being implemented

The intended destination is a universal unconditional material floor. During the capacity-limited transition, a Providence consociation guarantees only what its real institutions and resources can supply to people who freely join and genuinely participate in its shared life.

Participation begins through a moon-cycle practice of clarifying values, intentions, desires, commitments, and desired impact, through the app or an accessible equivalent. Reflections and locally held physiological signals may contribute to personal development and accumulated COHERENCE. No biometric result, measured self-regulation, perceived coherence, productivity, usefulness, or judgment of moral worth establishes or removes the material guarantee. Illness, disability, dependence, caregiving, crisis, technological exclusion, and temporary incapacity remain protected. Voluntary withdrawal ends the guarantee.

Earned stewardship sits above provision. It confers bounded, contestable responsibility, not superior human standing, a more valuable vote, or a permanent governing class.

## Central finding

Providence should not begin by asking one legal entity or one token to perform every function. The lowest-risk first architecture separates five things:

1. A religious or charitable entity receives donations and grants for genuinely religious, educational, scientific, or charitable work and assists a legally valid charitable class.
2. A member-governed consociation defines participation, equal political voice, reciprocal obligations, incapacity protections, exit, and the limits of any member guarantee.
3. One or more operating cooperatives employ people, produce goods and services, steward land, and account for patronage under the rules that apply to real economic activity.
4. A participant-controlled data system holds reflections and physiological information under strict purpose limits, consent, minimization, access, deletion, security, and breach procedures.
5. COHERENCE begins as a nontransferable developmental and stewardship record. It does not begin as an investment, freely transferable token, fiat substitute, claim on pooled assets, or promise of appreciation.

This separation does not settle the final entity chart. It prevents charitable donations, member benefits, commercial labor, health data, political rights, and transferable value from being collapsed into one instrument before their obligations are understood.

## What the present law makes difficult

### Charity cannot simply become a closed member subsidy

Federal 501(c)(3) organizations cannot be organized or operated for designated private interests, and charitable relief ordinarily must benefit a sufficiently large or indefinite charitable class rather than a preselected set of members. A church or religious organization may qualify under 501(c)(3), but the religious form does not erase private-benefit limits. A member-only material guarantee therefore should not be funded indiscriminately from restricted charitable assets. Charitable funds may support qualifying relief, education, religion, research, or a public charitable class; reciprocal member provisioning needs its own analysis and usually its own books or entity.

### Nonprofit status and tax exemption are different facts

California recognizes public benefit, mutual benefit, and religious nonprofit corporations. Formation as a nonprofit does not itself grant state tax exemption. A mutual benefit structure can serve members, while public benefit and religious structures carry different asset and purpose constraints. Any pilot must choose the form from its actual activity rather than calling the whole architecture a church or nonprofit and assuming the label resolves taxation or private benefit.

### Participation can become labor

People may volunteer freely to a religious, charitable, civic, or humanitarian nonprofit without expecting compensation. They generally cannot be treated as volunteers in ordinary commercial activities, and provision tied to productive work may be compensation regardless of the project's language. The pilot must distinguish spiritual or civic practice, member governance, volunteer service, employment, cooperative patronage, and independent enterprise. It must not use COHERENCE or access to necessities to hide wages.

### Transferable COHERENCE creates regulatory exposure

Federal money-transmission guidance distinguishes users from administrators and exchangers of convertible virtual currency. Accepting and transmitting value that substitutes for currency can make an administrator or exchanger a money transmitter. California separately regulates digital financial assets and money transmission, although some closed-loop stored value receives different treatment. Barter exchanges can trigger tax reporting. A crypto asset offered with promises of managerial work and financial return can form part of an investment contract even if the asset has another stated use.

For the first stage, COHERENCE should therefore remain nontransferable, nonredeemable for fiat, nonmarketed for appreciation, and unable to purchase third-party goods. Provision should flow from transparent budgets and member policies, not from a claim that a newly minted unit already carries real material value. Any later conversion, transfer, redemption, custody, exchange, or secondary market requires fresh money-transmission, digital-asset, tax, accounting, consumer-protection, and securities review before launch.

### Biomarker optimization can cross health and privacy boundaries

An app combining reflections with heart rate, heart-rate variability, pulse information, or connected-device data may fall within the Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule even when HIPAA does not apply. California treats health information and identifying biometric information as sensitive personal information. The FDA's general-wellness policy covers low-risk functions that encourage a healthy lifestyle without diagnosing, treating, curing, mitigating, or preventing disease. Claims that the system identifies illness, prescribes recovery, performs pulse diagnosis, or guides a person back to health may move beyond general wellness and require clinical and device review.

The first pilot should describe signals as experimental reflective inputs, not diagnoses or validated measures of moral, relational, or political truth. Raw signals should remain local by default. Any derived result must be contestable, purpose-limited, and separable from participation and provision. No model should infer a third person's health improvement without that person's informed consent and an evidence standard established before the claim is used.

## Recommended stages

### Stage 0: Doctrine, controls, and legal map

- Adopt the staged-floor invariant and definitions before promising benefits.
- Define participation, voluntary withdrawal, incapacity protection, equal voting rights, stewardship limits, complaints, appeal, and removal from a particular stewardship role.
- Treat the moon-cycle practice as one route and provide accessible non-digital and non-biometric equivalents.
- Map every proposed flow of fiat, goods, labor, data, authority, and COHERENCE.
- Obtain California nonprofit, cooperative, employment, privacy, health-product, tax, money-transmission, and securities advice against that exact map.
- Make no material guarantee until its goods, provider, budget, reserve, jurisdiction, scarcity policy, and remedy are published.

Exit condition: approved governance and data charters, written counsel issue list, no public claim that the floor or currency already exists.

### Stage 1: Fiat-funded practice and relief pilot

- Operate the moon-cycle reflection and relationship practice without transferable COHERENCE.
- Record COHERENCE only as a participant-controlled, nonfinancial developmental history.
- Use charitable funds only for qualifying exempt activities and valid charitable relief. Keep donor restrictions and charitable-class determinations explicit.
- Offer no member material guarantee beyond cash and in-kind resources already committed to a disclosed budget.
- Publish receipts, restricted and unrestricted balances, decision rights, conflicts, complaints, data incidents, and unmet demand without exposing participant health data.

Exit condition: one complete cycle of audited finances, consent and deletion tests, accessibility testing, adverse-event review, and truthful reporting of what could not be provided.

### Stage 2: Member consociation and limited guarantee

- Establish the member-governed body under the form counsel finds appropriate.
- Define active participation prospectively and behaviorally. Do not define it by biometric score or subjective approval by leaders.
- Adopt continuity protections so willing members remain covered through illness, disability, caregiving, crisis, technological exclusion, and temporary incapacity.
- Fund a narrow guarantee, such as meals, emergency shelter support, or essential-care grants, only after the resources are reserved.
- State duration, quantity, scarcity allocation, review, appeal, and the remedy when the institution cannot perform.

Exit condition: the limited guarantee survives a published stress test and an independent accessibility, labor, finance, and governance review.

### Stage 3: Productive cooperative layer

- Form operating cooperatives where members actually produce food, housing services, care, technology, education, or land stewardship.
- Pay wages where employment law requires them. Use patronage accounting only for genuine cooperative business under a preexisting obligation and qualified tax advice.
- Keep one-member-one-vote or another expressly approved equal political rule separate from task delegation and stewardship qualifications.
- Let COHERENCE inform voluntary development and narrowly relevant stewardship review, never wage suppression, necessities, general membership, or vote weight.

Exit condition: operating revenue covers a defined share of the guarantee without restricted charitable subsidy, and worker, member, and beneficiary accounts reconcile.

### Stage 4: Closed-loop economic experiment

- Consider limited redemption only for goods or services supplied by the issuer or an affiliate after written regulatory analysis.
- Publish issuance, expiration, redemption, reserves, valuation, tax treatment, error correction, fraud response, and insolvency rules.
- Prohibit cash redemption, third-party transmission, speculation, sale to investors, and secondary trading unless separately authorized after full review.
- Test whether the unit improves coordination more than ordinary accounting, member credits, or patronage records. Retire it if it adds surveillance or complexity without material benefit.

Exit condition: regulators and counsel have classified the actual instrument, the books reconcile to every outstanding claim, and participants can understand the system without doctrinal language.

### Stage 5: Inter-consociation federation

- Federate only institutions that can prove governance, reserves, data protection, accessibility, complaints, and local provision.
- Exchange goods and mutual aid through contracts and transparent budgets before exchanging a currency.
- Keep universal unconditional provision as the destination, expanding by demonstrated productive capacity and public partnership rather than declaration.

Exit condition: multiple independent consociations can honor reciprocal obligations through scarcity without centralizing biometric or political control.

## Minimum institutional invariants

- No biometric or coherence threshold controls the material guarantee.
- Active participation has an accessible equivalent and an objective appeal route.
- Temporary incapacity never becomes voluntary withdrawal by administrative presumption.
- Voluntary withdrawal requires clear notice and a transition period established before a crisis.
- Equal political voice means equal vote weight. Stewardship authority is delegated, bounded, reviewable, and removable.
- Raw physiological data remains participant-held by default and is never sold or used for advertising.
- A person may refuse biometrics without losing participation, provision, membership, or political voice.
- COHERENCE begins nontransferable and nonfinancial.
- No charitable entity promises benefits outside its exempt purpose or valid charitable class.
- No commercial labor is disguised as volunteering, spiritual practice, or currency accumulation.
- No guarantee exceeds committed resources. Failure triggers notice, review, appeal, and a published recovery process.
- Every stage publishes what exists, what remains proposed, who holds authority, and which evidence could falsify the design.

## Proof program

Before claiming that Providence outperforms an extractive economy, a pilot should publish at least:

- retention and voluntary withdrawal, separated from inability to participate;
- unmet food, shelter, care, and accessibility needs;
- distribution of benefits and stewardship by disability, income, race, gender, age, and other lawful equity measures;
- participant-reported coercion, exclusion, and ability to refuse biometrics;
- false positive, false negative, missing-data, and cross-population performance for every physiological inference;
- financial reserves, concentration of funding, restricted assets, operating revenue, and benefit cost;
- labor classification, wages, patronage, volunteer roles, and uncompensated work;
- voting equality, delegated authority, appeals, reversals, complaints, and removals;
- privacy incidents, deletion performance, model changes, and third-party access;
- land, food, ecological, and community outcomes with a stated baseline and limitations.

The proof is not that COHERENCE balances rise. The proof is that people have more secure access to necessities, more agency, better health and relational outcomes without being coerced to disclose private information, more equal political standing, and institutions that remain solvent and accountable under stress.

## Open work before any pilot promise

The author has settled the doctrine, not the operating institution. Counsel and affected participants still need to decide the entity chart, location, goods, budgets, reserve ratios, charitable classes, membership process, withdrawal transition, scarcity allocation, remedies, labor relationships, data controller, clinical boundaries, COHERENCE ontology, and whether any later transferability should exist at all.
