## Debt

Volume VIII Appendix B carried an internal production note that named two unfinished obligations. The note was removed from canonical prose on 2026-07-25 because bracketed annotations are production metadata rather than reader prose, and because no speech provider narrates them. Removing the note must not erase what it tracked.

Two obligations survive it:

1. The statistics under "The state of 2026 (§3)" are time-stamped to mid-2026. They were written as provisional and should be refreshed before the volume is treated as final.
2. The forward door remains set to Vol VII, the Seven Initiates of a BOW, where the "second meaning" named in §4 and the work of intimacy are meant to open fully. That handoff is asserted in Volume VIII but not yet fulfilled.

## Evidence

The removed note read: "Figures in §3 are time-stamped to mid-2026 and should be refreshed before publication. The forward door remains set to Vol VII, the Seven Initiates of a BOW, where §4's 'second meaning' and the work of intimacy open fully."

The affected figures are the war, economy, AI and labor, climate, overdose and suicide, DNA, social credit, fertility and loneliness, and trust entries under "The state of 2026 (§3)", plus the sex recession statistics under "Intimacy and the commodified body (§4)". Each is sourced but dated.

This mirrors CTD-0105, where a Volume I production note named obligations that CTD-0050 preserves separately. The same pattern applies here: the note is gone, the work is not.

## Paydown criteria

- C1. Every dated statistic under "The state of 2026 (§3)" is either refreshed against its cited source or explicitly marked as an as-of figure with its date in the prose.
- C2. The sex recession figures under "Intimacy and the commodified body (§4)" receive the same treatment.
- C3. The Vol VII forward door is either fulfilled by published Volume VII content or restated in Volume VIII as an acknowledged forward reference rather than a pending handoff.
- C4. No bracketed production note is reintroduced to carry any of this work.
- C5. Appendix B uses one abbreviation style throughout. The 2026-07-25 audio pass expanded ampersands, tildes, percent signs, and slash pairs under "The state of 2026 (§3)" and "Intimacy and the commodified body (§4)", but left the preceding source bullets in the compact form, so the appendix currently mixes both.
