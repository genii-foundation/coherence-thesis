# Coherence Thesis Corpus Voice Card

This card supplies the shared editorial floor for all nine volumes. Each volume card inherits these rules and records only its identity, local register, specific departures, lexicon, images, controls, risks, and approval state.

## Corpus rules

- Relationship to the reader: Invite scrutiny, questions, practice, or participation. Never conscript agreement, diagnose doubt, claim access to private experience, or tell readers what they feel.
- Cadence: Preserve meaningful variation between compact propositions and longer argument-bearing sentences. Short sentences must mark a real hinge or landing. Do not flatten cumulative reasoning into uniform declaratives merely because the original sentence is long.
- Emotional temperature: Let urgency, gravity, warmth, reverence, or hope arise from the argument and evidence. Never manufacture uplift in a closing sentence, inflate uncertainty into certainty, or use intimacy as a substitute for warrant.

## Authority

- Scope: These rules bind every editorial pass, validator, and calibration bench unless a volume card records a narrower or stronger rule.
- Precedence: A volume card may specify a local degree, rhythm, or temperature. It may not release the corpus floor without an explicit author ruling recorded in durable evidence.
- Canon boundary: This card governs editorial craft. It does not settle doctrine, factual claims, claim scope, project status, or canon.
