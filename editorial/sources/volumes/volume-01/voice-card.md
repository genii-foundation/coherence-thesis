# Volume I Voice Card

## Corpus inheritance

- Inherits: `../../corpus/voice-card.md`
- Where this volume departs: It moves more slowly than the later volumes, makes admissions of uncertainty load bearing, and preserves threshold pacing even after releasing the original display glyph.

## Identity

- Volume: I, *Humanity's Most Viable Future*
- Central thesis: A viable post-extractive civilization must treat coherence as a biological condition of durable coordination. Providence, PURPOSEFUL, the Currency of Presence, and locally sovereign Scales are proposed as a falsifiable architecture built on that condition.
- Intended reader: A skeptical general reader willing to follow philosophical, biological, and institutional claims without specialist preparation.
- Relationship to the reader: Invitational and candid. The narrator welcomes scrutiny, names the architecture's danger, and refuses to demand belief. This is the volume's governing constraint. An edit that converts an invitation into an assertion is a defect under `R-VOICE-BIND` however much it improves the sentence.
- Narrative stance: First person plural for shared civilizational responsibility, with occasional direct address when the invitation genuinely requires it.
- Function in the series: The volume that must earn a hearing. It establishes the biological premise every later volume assumes, and it is the only volume a reader is guaranteed to have read. It can afford to move slowly.
- Default intervention level: Level 2 line edit. Level 3 is appropriate for repeated qualifications, duplicated summaries, and structural scaffolding that obscures the argument. Level 4 requires explicit approval and may not be reached by accumulating sentence-level decisions.
- Expected compression: 10 to 18 percent against the baseline. This volume carries little duplicated structure, so a large reduction indicates register loss rather than removed surplus. A batch outside this range must justify itself under `R-PROPORTION` before it is recorded.

## Register

- Dominant register: Philosophical argument in plain language, carried by an organic developmental metaphor.
- Permitted register changes: Analytical in the Seed, Sprout, and Stem; reflective in the Soil; visionary but falsifiable in the Flower; intimate in Maren's vignette; invitational in the close.
- Direct address override: Light. Use direct address to orient or invite.
- Degree of abstraction: Moderate. Project terms may remain abstract when they name a real distinction, but nearby verbs and examples must show what they do.
- Emotional temperature override: Sober urgency that opens into earned hope through the preceding argument.
- Humor and irony: Minimal. A restrained turn such as "The attempt is small, and knows it" may remain when it releases pressure without trivializing the claim.
- Admissions of uncertainty: Load bearing. Where the narrator concedes that a method is not yet known or a claim is not yet proven, the concession is part of the invitation and is not surplus to be tightened away.

## Cadence

- Cadence override: Alternate compact propositions with longer cumulative reasoning. Short sentences may mark a true hinge, not simulate revelation.
- Characteristic paragraph movement: Claim, mechanism, consequence. High-register passages often move from biological observation through an organic image into institutional implication.
- Cumulative sentences are structural: A long sentence with a clear spine, particularly the `not by X, but by Y` construction, carries the volume's central turns. Splitting one into declaratives is a defect under `R-CADENCE` unless the sentence is genuinely hard to follow rather than merely long.
- Permitted fragments: Title matter, definitions, deliberate section thresholds, and a few brief lines whose silence carries structural weight.
- Permitted ritual repetition: Seed, sprout, stem, soil, and flower may recur as structural memory. Repetition of caveats, conclusions, and "this book" announcements should be reduced.
- Read-aloud qualities to preserve: Breath at thresholds, the gradual descent into the Soil, the lift into the Flower, the steadiness of the surveillance safeguards, and the final return to tending the seed.
- Threshold pacing: The baseline marked 45 thresholds with a display glyph. The glyph is released by author decision of 2026-07-29. The pacing it encoded is not. Where a threshold fell, the paragraph boundary and the change of breath must remain.

## Language

- Canonical terms: coherence; Providence; PURPOSEFUL; Currency of Presence; COHERENCE; Bio-Consensus; the Doors; ICONS; Scale; Cardinal Scale.
- Terms with volume-specific meanings: substrate means the conditions that make coordination possible; presencing means a relational event in which nervous systems settle and synchronize; flower means the visible architecture arising from developmental ground.
- Signature diction: seed, root, soil, flower, substrate, regulation, relationship, trust, nervous system, living process, tending, connective tissue.
- Diction that belongs to other volumes and should not migrate here: Detailed protocol language, dense implementation taxonomies, numerological exposition, and liturgical language not already grounded in Volume I.
- Recurrent phrases that are intentional: Technology amplifies the state it is given; wisdom exchanged for future; the currency can raise a ceiling but never lower a floor; before a thousand, one; the seed has been planted.
- Recurrent phrases that should be reduced: This book, what follows, not X but Y as a reflex rather than a turn, for the first time in history, the question is, and repeated assurances that the work is not a manifesto.
- Deixis: Where the volume names a condition the reader is living inside, prefer the proximal demonstrative under `R-DEIXIS`. The definite article converts a live pressure into a settled fact.
- Display punctuation: Preserve the ampersand in `Seed, Sprout, Stem & Soil`. The compact bond is part of the heading's pace and identity. Do not expand ampersands mechanically in titles or display matter.

## Images and structure

- Signature metaphors: The argument grows from seed through flower; intelligence is a flame; civilization is a living process; Providence is connective tissue among Scales; empirical testing is the load test beneath an architecture.
- Recurring images and motifs: Ground, roots, bodily regulation, rooms returning to themselves, trust traveling, cells becoming a body, and a material floor built through participation toward universal provision.
- Protected structural devices: The five organic movements; the Pause and Threshold; the Maren vignette; the surveillance invariants; the falsifiability section; the closing return to the seed.
- Images that must not be explained away: A room brought back to itself; the attempt is small, and knows it; a Scale as one whole cell; the surface slowly becoming a body; the seed depending on those who tend it.
- Mixed or exhausted metaphors to watch: Architecture, substrate, ground, flower, network, connective tissue, ledger, and currency can collide when too many appear in one paragraph.

## Controls

- Exemplar passages that define the voice, anchored by opening words: "The Coherence Thesis is an invitation to recognize"; "The technology does not choose"; "It is wisdom exchanged for future"; the bridge-load analogy opening "How Coherence Becomes Structure"; Maren's frightened-room vignette; the floor and ceiling invariant; the closing invitation to tend the soil.
- Exemplar opening anchors: "The Coherence Thesis is an invitation to recognize"; "The technology does not choose"; "It is wisdom exchanged for future"; "With the constraints now clearly stated"; "Imagine someone who has spent years quietly good at something"; "Providence cannot yet offer everyone a material floor"; "This is the invitation: to do it knowingly"
- Protected lines or passages: "It is wisdom exchanged for future."; "The technology does not choose. The conditions surrounding it choose."; "the currency may support reflection and voluntary trust, but it may never become the price of bread, belonging, or a vote."; "Before a thousand, one."; "The seed has been planted. What it becomes depends on who tends it."
- Quotation provenance: The Maren anchor is a compression ratified with the card's 2026-07-29 rewrite. The material-floor anchor and protected bread, belonging, and vote sentence are post-baseline doctrine added under CTD-0005. They replace the formerly protected unconditional-floor language, which the 2026-08-12 ruling limits to the intended destination rather than the capacity-limited beginning.
- How edits to this volume characteristically fail: By converting the invitation into a constraint; by splitting cumulative sentences into uniform declaratives and calling it clarity; by removing threshold pacing as ornament; by tightening away the narrator's admissions of uncertainty; and by escalating a bounded claim into a universal in exchange for a shorter phrase. Each of these leaves every sentence true and the volume unrecognizable.
- Known weak passages: Claims that announce their own importance; repeated limitations before the thesis advances; exact empirical assertions without visible sources; accumulations of three abstractions; repeated meta references to the book; and headings inherited from the drafting process.
- Known factual or logical concerns: The claims about physiological detection, group performance, nervous-system invariance, modernity's historical break, thermodynamic collapse, and the uniqueness of Providence need a separate sourced fact review.
- Open author decisions: Whether to restore a section called The Roots once its bibliography exists; whether COHERENCE is literally identical with the relation it records or is the portable record of that relation; whether the phrase "for the first time in history" should remain where source evidence cannot establish priority. These are canon. No editorial pass may resolve one to make a sentence land.
