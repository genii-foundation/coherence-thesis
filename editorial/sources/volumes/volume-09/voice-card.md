# Volume IX Voice Card

## Corpus inheritance

- Inherits: `../../corpus/voice-card.md`
- Where this volume departs: It works as a compact builder's manual, permits moderate direct address in the invitation, and repeatedly qualifies technical detail so proposal never impersonates implementation.

## Identity

- Volume: IX, *The Cardinal Scale: The First of Many ICONS*.
- Central thesis: the series must return from argument to one bounded place where its institutional design can be tested without allowing detail, symbolism, or invitation to impersonate proof.
- Intended reader: builders, skeptics, participants, stewards, lawyers, engineers, clinicians, organizers, and communities asked to bear the proposal's risks.
- Relationship to the reader: practical and invitational, but judgment comes before allegiance. The reader may refuse the design without being cast as morally asleep.
- Narrative stance: compact recap, design specification, explicit uncertainty register, and lyrical closing grounded in water, seed, weather, and consequence.
- Default intervention level: Level 2, with Level 3 reconstruction where recap duplicated earlier volumes or architecture claims contradicted the manuscript's own open-problem list.

## Register

- Dominant register: a builder's manual tempered by philosophical and ecological prose.
- Permitted register changes: symbolic in the Mars opening; concise in the recap; technical in the architecture; constitutional at the social-credit boundary; lyrical around water, seed, and the smallest nest.
- Direct address override: Moderate in the invitation, always preserving refusal.
- Degree of abstraction: moderate to high. Every architecture term should return to a person, right, budget, remedy, resource, or place.
- Emotional temperature override: Determined without certainty and hopeful without claiming implementation.
- Humor and irony: minimal.

## Cadence

- Cadence override: Compact statements followed by qualifying sentences that expose what remains open.
- Characteristic paragraph movement: describe the intended function, name the risk, state the missing proof, and return to a bounded test.
- Permitted fragments: title display, two opening epigraphs, the paired questions, the closing Purposeful and Providence lines.
- Permitted ritual repetition: lay one stone; Scale by Scale; seed in drawer and ground; the smallest nest; people not yet born.
- Read-aloud qualities to preserve: the movement from Saturn to Mars, the spring and watershed image, the floor as moral center, the bounded promises, and the final seed passage.

## Language

- Canonical terms: Scale; ICONS; Providence; PURPOSEFUL; the Doors; the Membrane; the Currency of Presence; COHERENCE; Bio-Consensus; Proof of Council; Coherence Engine; participant-held record; the dragon named Earth.
- Terms with volume-specific meanings: the Cardinal Scale remains a composite; no physical place or living project may be named as its proposed first ground without primary verification and the affirmative support of its stewards; the Container is a class of legal and governance forms, not a settled entity; Land-NFT and SDAC remain earlier design labels outside the canonical stack; Coherent Resonance remains an inner quality from Volume VII rather than a wallet.
- Signature diction: plant, ground, watershed, spring, membrane, floor, stack, bounded promise, stone, seed, weather, shade, roots.
- Diction that belongs to other volumes and should not migrate here: numerology as evidence, spiritual authority, biometric certainty, apocalyptic accounting, or corporate pitch language.
- Recurrent phrases that are intentional: lay one stone; a proposal is still a proposal; detail without status can impersonate certainty; the floor is where the architecture reveals its moral center.
- Recurrent phrases that should be reduced: the unit is sound; the sequence is settled; the first ground exists; existence proof; already known; the only measure that matters. Note: the first four of these are the baseline's own committed assertions in its "What We Know" list. Their suppression is a claim-scope decision the pass made, not a settled rule; see Open author decisions.

## Images and structure

- Signature metaphors: Mars as force turned toward making; the Scale as a small complete expression; water gathering and returning; Providence as connective tissue; the moral floor; seed meeting weather.
- Recurring images and motifs: the spring, the watershed, another person downstream, unused room and visiting nurse, care at a dying mother's bedside, bees and saved seed, a room holding disagreement, a shared meal, shade and roots after rain.
- Protected structural devices: register note; five numbered sections; compact canonical recap; architecture stack; commitments and open-problems lists; bounded invitation; closing.
- Images that must not be explained away: spring returning water; seed in drawer and ground; the fork between a score and a floor; the forest beginning.
- Mixed or exhausted metaphors to watch: dragon, nervous system, flower, root, current, currency, wallet, engine, stack, and container. Technical terms must not crowd the land out of the chapter.

## Controls

- Exemplar passages that define the voice: the Mars register note; the water-centered Cardinal Scale; the mechanism of recognition; the social-credit fork; what we know and what we do not; the three people from Volume VIII; the final seed passage.
- Exemplar opening anchors: "Every volume has worn its own face"; "For most of this work the Cardinal Scale has been drawn as a composite"; "Recognition has a mechanism"; "And here the fork from Volume VIII becomes the knife's edge a builder must respect"; "We owe a builder honesty, because we are asking you to build and not merely to believe"; "Volume VIII left three people inside this Scale, and they are who we build for"; "A seed in a drawer is a story"
- Protected lines or passages: "The whole of it is offered as a supposition held with both hands."; "Providence offers only a third thing: recognition."; "A seed in a drawer is a story. A seed in the ground, tended by someone who chose it, is a forest beginning."
- Quotation provenance: every quoted string above is verbatim in the named baseline, in the corpus rendering convention, and stands in the current manuscript. Two protections were removed by 2026-07-31 author rulings: the pass-authored "A detailed proposal is still a proposal until people can test it safely and in public." was released under T-022 in favor of the baseline supposition sentence, and the baseline floor sentence was not adopted while CTD-0005 remained contested. - Known weak passages: the number and planet scheme; network-state comparison; site and population status; implementation invitations; every technical term without an owner, mechanism, or public record.
- Known factual or logical concerns: verification and steward support for any future proposed site; subsistence conditionality; biometric governance; COHERENCE ontology and lifecycle; Proof of Council; Entelechy; population thresholds; legal vessel; security; participation route; Fuller quotation.
- Open author decisions: the institutions and resources that can make the staged floor real; stewardship evidence for particular roles; whether Entelechy names the Membrane; final wallet terminology; retained orphan architecture labels; network status; final approval of the 40 percent developmental compression.
